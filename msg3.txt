fix: elevation for every engine start, real elevated restart, logs on open

The reported "strategy picking failed" was not the strategies. The log
showed Win32Exception 740 from winws.exe: the app tried to start an
executable with a requireAdministrator manifest without admin rights.

Three separate defects behind that single message:

1. Only the power button checked elevation. Strategy testing, hosts
   writing and autopilot just called Start() and got the raw .NET text
   "the requested operation requires elevation" into the log.
   EnsureElevation() is now the one entry point for every engine or
   System32 write, and it offers the restart instead of just failing.
   WinwsSupervisor also catches 740 specifically and writes a sentence a
   human can act on.

2. Elevated restart did not start anything. The new process acquired the
   single-instance mutex, found the old process still alive (it lives a
   few hundred milliseconds after starting its replacement), signalled it
   and quit. The app simply never appeared. Now RestartElevated passes
   --restarted and TryAcquire waits up to 8 s for the mutex.

3. The Logs screen opened empty until "Refresh" was pressed - showScreen
   requested lists and proxy but never logs.

Also from the same report:
- Hosts and "Lists from the internet" were hidden in simple mode, because
  the whole cards were marked data-advanced. Updating hosts is a normal
  feature, not an advanced one. Unhidden, and the button is now called
  "Update" instead of "Apply" - it downloads sources, merges and writes.
- Every diagnostics step now states roughly how long it takes: ~10 s for
  the 12 checks, ~20-40 s for the baseline probe, 2-5 min for strategy
  picking. A button that says nothing looks hung after 30 seconds.
- ROADMAP records the owner's decision on engines: the built-in engine
  will be a third option, winws/winws2 stay forever as fallback, engine
  choice is exposed in the UI, and autopilot/presets must be able to
  switch between engines, not only between strategies.

selftest, CI and the encoding check are green; the UI was verified in the
browser (Hosts visible in simple mode, time estimates render, button
renamed).
