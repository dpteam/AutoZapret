feat: readiness preflight and a non-modal "no admin" banner

Two ideas that close the same gap: the user presses, nothing happens, and
the app does not say why.

Preflight (~200 ms, before winws starts):
  engine present; winws.exe / winws2.exe; WinDivert64.sys and
  WinDivert.dll; cygwin1.dll for v1; a foreign winws holding WinDivert;
  the selected strategy exists and produces arguments; every referenced
  .bin exists AND passes PayloadValidator; every hostlist/ipset file
  exists; lua files for zapret2; admin rights.

Why each of those matters rather than being decoration:
- winws does not validate .bin contents, it just sends bytes. A missing
  or corrupt fake means a strategy that "runs" and does nothing.
- a missing hostlist makes winws complain on stderr while the UI shows
  "enabled", which is the worst possible feedback.
- a foreign winws (the zapret service) holds WinDivert and our start
  silently cannot filter.

Blockers cancel the start, warnings do not. The list goes to the UI, so
the message is "missing cygwin1.dll", not "failed to start".

Banner instead of a modal: being non-elevated is a persistent condition,
not an event. A dialog on every power click trains people to dismiss
dialogs without reading them. The home screen now always shows
"Running without administrator rights" with one button, and
EnsureElevation just highlights it and notifies the tray.

selftest: 11 preflight checks, including that an empty strategy id is
not an error (strategy testing passes none on purpose) while an unknown
one is a blocker.

Verified in the browser: the banner renders with the restart button and
the problem list, both from state and from a preflight message.
